Jarvis - Privacy Policy

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant built and used by one person, Karan Chugani, for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

Jarvis connects to Karan's own Google account (Gmail and Google Calendar) to read mail, read and add calendar events, and send mail he approves. Data is accessed only on Karan's own computers, is not shared with anyone, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to train models. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on those computers.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
